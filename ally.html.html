Why free, with no limits

Good help shouldn't depend on who can afford it. A student studying for an exam and someone running a household budget deserve the same quality of answer.

Ally stays open to everyone — no subscription tier, no locked-away "pro" features, no cap on how many questions you ask in a day.

How memory works right now

Tap "Remember me" during a chat and Ally gives you a short phrase and saves your conversation on this device only — no account, nothing sent anywhere. Come back on the same phone or browser, and it's still there.

Making that phrase work from any device — so a memory started on your phone picks up on a library computer — needs a real server and database behind the site. That's the natural next step once this is ready to launch for real.
